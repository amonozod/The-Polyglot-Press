About us

Curiosity has no single language.

The Polyglot Press is a student-led magazine created to give students a platform to investigate ideas, ask difficult questions, and share their perspectives with a wider audience.

We started with a simple complaint: school publications usually read like announcements. Ours doesn't have to. Every piece here begins with a student who noticed something — a pattern, a contradiction, a question nobody had bothered to ask out loud — and followed it further than a five-paragraph essay usually allows.

We publish research, interviews, essays, opinion writing, and fiction from every discipline, not just the humanities. A good idea doesn't check which department it came from.

What we value

Student writing, first

Every article, interview, and script is written and edited by students, from pitch to publication.

Independent research

We run our own surveys, interviews, and data collection rather than repeating what's already been said.

Cross-disciplinary thinking

Science, law, art, and economics all get equal space — the best stories rarely stay in one lane.

Intellectual curiosity

We'd rather publish a well-asked question than a confidently wrong answer.

Creativity

Fiction, design, and visual storytelling sit alongside reporting, not apart from it.

Open discussion

Comments, ratings, and letters are part of the publication, not an afterthought bolted on.
